## 💡 Engineering Ethos & Philosophy

- **Beyond the Happy Path**: Systems are built not just for when everything goes right, but for how gracefully they handle network partitions, rate limits, corrupted payloads, and high-concurrency pressure.
- **Security from the Inside Out**: Deep offensive security knowledge translates into threat-hardened software design, rigorous input validation, safe credential handling, and defensible infrastructure.
- **Production Retrieval over Demo Chatbots**: LLM engineering should focus on observable, evaluated, secure retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and structured autonomous agents rather than trivial prompt-wrapper demos.
- **High Concurrency & Low Latency**: Leveraging asynchronous paradigms (`AsyncIO`, `FastAPI`, `Celery`, `Redis`, `WebSockets`) to achieve maximum throughput with minimal resource footprint.
